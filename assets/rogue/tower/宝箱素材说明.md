Detailed beautiful crisp pixel clusters and stepped outlines, hand-crafted fantasy dungeon RPG art. Teal shadow colors, amber gold highlights. No floor, no background scene, no cast shadow, no text, no labels, no UI, no people, no coins outside chest. Both states are recognizable and dramatic at large scale, not small icons. Output landscape 2:1 sprite sheet.
