## 正文

以下正文均使用上方夜间酒馆背景，立绘随说话人物切换。

### 1. 【旁白】

Lu Chuan washes the stone dust from his hands. Lyse sets the wrapped fragment in the middle of the counter.

陆川洗净手上的石尘，莉瑟将包着碎晶的布放在吧台中央。

### 2. 【艾莉娅】

The sign has not been turned yet. Place the fragment beside the crack, and we will open once it is safe.

门牌还没翻。先把碎片放到裂口旁，确认安全以后再开门。

### 3. 【陆川】

The shape matches. It seems to be moving toward the gap on its own.

形状刚好能对上。它好像自己在往缺口里靠。

### 4. 【旁白】

The fragment joins the first crack, and light spreads slowly across the surface. Deeper fractures remain clearly visible.

碎晶融入第一道裂痕，微光沿着石面慢慢铺开。更深的裂缝仍清晰可见。

### 5. 【言契系统】

The first structural repair is complete. The seal is becoming stable, but is not fully restored.

第一处结构修复完成。封印趋于稳定，尚未完全恢复。

### 6. 【莉瑟】

The butterfly no longer circles this gap. At least today, we found the right piece.

光蝶不再绕着这个缺口打转了。至少今天，我们找对了一块。

### 7. 【旁白】

Lu Chuan washes his hands, and Aelia reheats the soup. Lyse lights the table lamps, and the sign turns to open for the first time.

陆川洗净手，艾莉娅重新热好汤。莉瑟点亮桌灯，门牌第一次翻到营业的一面。

### 8. 【旁白】

The delivery worker returns after his work, followed by two patrol guards. The four prepared round tables finally welcome customers.

送货人收工后回来，两名巡逻守卫随后进门。准备好的四张圆桌终于迎来了客人。

### 9. 【送货人】

One bowl of soup without eggs, and two slices of bread to take away. I remember you said you would open only when ready.

一碗不加鸡蛋的汤，两片面包带走。我记得你说过，准备好了才开门。

### 10. 【陆川】

Soup without eggs, bread packed to go. This is our first service. Thank you for coming.

汤不加鸡蛋，面包打包。今晚是第一次营业，谢谢你愿意来。

### 11. 【艾莉娅】

I will pack the bread while you serve the soup. Keep the entrance and kitchen passage clear, and follow the order we practiced.

我来装面包，你先端汤。门口和厨房的通道都留空，照我们试过的顺序来。

### 12. 【旁白】

Spoons and conversation gradually fill the hall. The crystal glows steadily, but no further crack disappears on its own.

碗勺声和谈笑声渐渐填满大厅。晶石的微光稳定下来，但没有新的裂缝自行消失。

### 13. 【陆川】

Next, the ledger. Today's sales brought in twenty-four copper coins. Ingredients cost eighteen.

接下来是账本。今日营业收了二十四枚铜币，食材成本十八枚。

### 14. 【艾莉娅】

That leaves six in profit, not twenty-four. We need supplies tomorrow, so do not treat all revenue as spending money.

那是六枚盈余，不是二十四枚。明天还要进货，别把收入全当能花的钱。

### 15. 【陆川】

Noted: keep costs and revenue separate. I still have the twelve coins left from our supply budget.

记下了，成本和收入分开。我还留着采购后剩下的十二枚备用金。

### 16. 【莉瑟】

Clear accounts on day one are more reliable than a fortune. Will you be open tomorrow?

第一天能算清楚，比第一天就赚大钱可靠。你们明天还开门吗？

### 17. 【陆川】

Yes. We will keep today's menu. Selling one pot of soup does not make me a master chef.

开。先保留今天的菜单，不能因为卖完一锅汤就假装自己是大厨。

### 18. 【艾莉娅】

Copy the mine notes too. We will give them to the watch officer tomorrow, rather than keeping the danger in our own notebook.

也把矿道记录抄一份。明早交给值守官，不把危险只留在我们自己的本子里。

### 19. 【旁白】

Wheels sound outside. An attendant in a dark cloak stops beneath the porch.

门外传来车轮声，一位披深色斗篷的侍从在门廊下停住。

### 20. 【侍从】

Your Highness, the carriage is ready. The hour you asked us to wait for has arrived.

殿下，马车已备好。您让我们等到的时刻已经到了。

### 21. 【陆川】

Your Highness? So the crest on the handle was not decoration included with the lamp.

殿下？所以灯柄上的纹章，不是买灯时附送的装饰。

### 22. 【莉瑟】

No. I am sorry. Today I used a name that gave me a little freedom.

不是。对不起，今天先说了一个能让我自在些的名字。

### 23. 【陆川】

What you told us about the lantern and mine was true. The rest of your name can wait until you want to share it.

你告诉我们的灯和矿道都是真的。名字的另一半，等你想说时再说。

### 24. 【莉瑟】

Thank you. Next time, I would still like this seat and a bowl of your soup.

谢谢。下次我还是想坐今天这个位置，喝你们的汤。

### 25. 【旁白】

The carriage crosses the stone bridge. Aelia puts away the last cup, and Lu Chuan turns the sign to 'closed'.

马车越过石桥。艾莉娅收好最后一只杯子，陆川将门牌翻到打烊的一面。

### 26. 【艾莉娅】

If you still need someone to guard this place, I will be here tomorrow too. I was not waiting only for the roof to be repaired.

如果你还需要人守着这里，我明天也在。不是只等屋顶修好的那一天。

### 27. 【陆川】

I do. We will plan tomorrow together. Tonight, let us get some rest.

需要。明天的分工还是一起商量，今天就先好好休息。

### 28. 【旁白】

The ledger holds the shopping list, the first paid orders, and the mine notes. Only tonight has the waystation completed its first service.

账本里夹着采购单、第一批订单和矿道记录。直到这一晚，驿站才真正完成第一次营业。

### 29. 【陆川】

Leave the light by the door on. If someone passes at night, they will know there is still a place to return to.

门口那盏灯留着吧。要是有人夜里路过，至少知道这里还有个归处。

### 30. 【旁白】

Deep underground, a whisper travels through the darkness: 'That waystation... it is shining again.'

地下深处，一道低语穿过黑暗：“那间驿站……又亮起来了。”

## 打烊：艾莉娅留下的理由

![背景图](D:/WORK/Cet6Story/chapters/art/tavern/chapter-one-stay-closeup.png)

### 1. 【旁白】

After closing, Lu Chuan puts away the ledger. Aelia remains beside the mine notes, studying the repaired crystal.

打烊之后，陆川收起账本。艾莉娅却仍站在矿道记录旁，目光停在那枚修补过的晶核上。

### 2. 【陆川】

Thanks for everything today. But shouldn't you be continuing your journey tomorrow?

今天多亏你了。不过，你明天是不是也该继续赶路了？

### 3. 【艾莉娅】

We still haven't solved the mystery in the mine. Why was that fragment there, and why did the crystal respond to it? I want to stay and find out.

矿道里的事还没查清。那块碎片为什么会在那里，晶核又为什么会回应它……我想留下弄明白。

### 4. 【陆川】

So you're staying to investigate?

所以，是为了调查？

![背景图](D:/WORK/Cet6Story/chapters/art/tavern/chapter-one-stay-closeup.png)

### 5. 【艾莉娅】

And you didn't try to be a hero today. You worked with us and listened when it was time to retreat. I feel safer working with someone like that.

还有，你今天没有逞强。该配合的时候配合，该撤退的时候也听得进去。和这样的人一起做事，我比较放心。

### 6. 【陆川】

Then shall I save you some breakfast tomorrow?

那明天，还是给你留一份早饭？

### 7. 【艾莉娅】

Please do. You're wiping the tables, though. Don't leave them all to me again.

留吧。桌子你擦，别又全推给我。

### 8. 【旁白】

Lu Chuan picks up the cloth as Aelia folds the notes. Unanswered questions and a trusted partner give her a reason to stay. The lamp by the door is still burning.

陆川拿起抹布，艾莉娅把记录折好。留下来的理由有了答案：未解的线索，还有一个值得信任的搭档。门边的灯，仍然亮着。
