# 第一章第一节：雨夜追踪

> 原创四六级听力风格训练；313 个英文词，24 句，4 道穿插题。

## 场景与美术

陈星遥沿用白发、紫黑长风衣像素全身立绘。背景为同色系像素雨巷，主线修复能力直接可用。

## 对白与题目

**1. 旁白**

Rain strikes the street. A red light follows me through the smoke.

雨水敲打空荡的街道。一束红光穿过烟雾，紧紧追着我。

**2. 陈星遥**

Keep your head down. That screen is scanning your face, not showing an advertisement.

低下头。那块屏幕在扫描你的脸，不是在播放广告。

**3. 陆川**

Why would it scan me? I have only just arrived in this city.

它为什么要扫描我？我才刚来到这座城市。

**4. 陈星遥**

There is no record of your identity. Here, that makes you a security risk.

系统里没有你的身份记录。在这里，这就意味着你是安全风险。

**5. 陆川**

So I am in trouble because the system cannot find my name?

所以，系统找不到我的名字，就认为我有问题？

**6. 陈星遥**

Exactly. Follow me before the patrol arrives. Ask questions somewhere safer.

没错。巡逻队赶来前跟我走。找个安全的地方再问。

### 听力题 1 · 身份原因

Why is Lu Chuan being treated as a security risk?

- A. He has damaged a public screen.
- B. He has entered a restricted shop.
- C. His identity cannot be found in the records.
- D. He has refused to speak to the patrol.

答案：C。第 4–5 句说明，陆川没有身份记录，因此被视为安全风险；不是因为他破坏设备或拒绝巡逻队。

**7. 陆川**

The main station is nearby. Could we ask the staff for assistance there?

主车站就在附近。我们能去找工作人员帮忙吗？

**8. 陈星遥**

Every entrance requires official permission. Without it, the gates will report your location.

每个入口都需要正式授权。没有权限，闸机会报告你的位置。

**9. 陆川**

What is our alternative? This street seems blocked at both ends.

那还有什么办法？这条街的两端似乎都被封锁了。

**10. 陈星遥**

An old maintenance passage runs below the shops. It leads to an abandoned platform.

商店下面有一条旧维修通道，通往废弃站台。

**11. 陆川**

Does the passage have cameras as well? I would rather avoid another alarm.

通道里也有摄像头吗？我可不想再触发一次警报。

**12. 陈星遥**

Not working ones. The equipment failed months ago. Nobody repaired it.

有，但早就坏了。设备几个月前就出了故障，也没人来修。

### 听力题 2 · 路线建议

What does Xingyao suggest they do?

- A. Ask the station staff for permission.
- B. Take an unused passage beneath the shops.
- C. Wait for someone to repair the cameras.
- D. Leave through the main station entrance.

答案：B。第 10 句建议走商店下方的维修通道；车站闸机会报告位置，所以不能从主入口走。

**13. 旁白**

A steel door blocks the stairs. She checks the terminal, then shakes her head.

一扇铁门挡住楼梯。她检查了终端，随后摇了摇头。

**14. 陈星遥**

The lock has power, but its control circuit is damaged. My tools cannot restore it.

锁还有电，但控制电路损坏了。我的工具修不好。

**15. 万象系统**

Local fault identified. Remote repair available. Confirm activation to restore the damaged circuit.

已识别局部故障。远程修复可用。确认启动，即可恢复损坏的电路。

**16. 陆川**

Confirmed. Stand back. I can handle this problem.

确认启动。离门远一点。我想，这个问题我能解决。

**17. 陈星遥**

It is opening. You did not even touch the terminal. What exactly did you do?

门开了。你连终端都没碰。你到底做了什么？

**18. 陆川**

Repaired the circuit. I will explain when we are no longer being followed.

修好了电路。等没人追我们的时候，我再解释。

### 听力题 3 · 故障细节

What prevents the steel door from opening at first?

- A. A damaged control circuit.
- B. A complete loss of electricity.
- C. A missing maintenance tool.
- D. A warning from the patrol.

答案：A。第 14 句明确说锁有电，但控制电路损坏。选项 B 把有电误写成完全断电。系统修复电路后门打开。

**19. 陈星遥**

Fair enough. I am Xingyao, a reporter. And I owe you an explanation too.

也对。我叫星遥，是记者。我也欠你一个解释。

**20. 陆川**

Lu Chuan. If you are a reporter, why are you hiding from the patrol?

陆川。如果你是记者，为什么要躲着巡逻队？

**21. 陈星遥**

I found evidence that citizens are losing their identities. My sister disappeared during the investigation.

我发现有居民的身份被删除。我妹妹在调查期间失踪了。

**22. 陆川**

You believe those events are connected, but you still need proof. Is that right?

你认为这些事有联系，但还需要证据。对吗？

**23. 陈星遥**

Yes. Help me reach the shelter. I will show you what I found.

对。帮我到地下避难所，我就给你看调查发现。

**24. 旁白**

She offers her hand. Behind us, the door closes before the red light returns.

她向我伸出手。红光再次扫来之前，身后的门已经合上。

### 听力题 4 · 人物目标

What is Xingyao mainly trying to do?

- A. Apply for a job at the station.
- B. Hide evidence from her sister.
- C. Repair the city cameras.
- D. Investigate deleted identities and find her sister.

答案：D。第 21–23 句表明星遥调查身份删除事件，并寻找失踪的妹妹。她需要证据，所以带男主去避难所继续调查。
