## 第一章：找回被抹去的名字

沿用陆川与陈星遥，四节连贯剧情。首节维持原有内容和存档ID；后三节各24句、3题，词汇与推断逐渐加深。主线道具价格120/180/240金币，共540金币，使用后永久激活，不重复收费。

## 第一卷 · 霓虹失名者 / 第一章 1.1 雨夜追踪
初到未来城市，被无身份记录的扫描系统追踪。

**旁白**：Rain strikes the street. A red light follows me through the smoke.

雨水敲打空荡的街道。一束红光穿过烟雾，紧紧追着我。

**陈星遥**：Keep your head down. That screen is scanning your face, not showing an advertisement.

低下头。那块屏幕在扫描你的脸，不是在播放广告。

**陆川**：Why would it scan me? I have only just arrived in this city.

它为什么要扫描我？我才刚来到这座城市。

**陈星遥**：There is no record of your identity. Here, that makes you a security risk.

系统里没有你的身份记录。在这里，这就意味着你是安全风险。

**陆川**：So I am in trouble because the system cannot find my name?

所以，系统找不到我的名字，就认为我有问题？

**陈星遥**：Exactly. Follow me before the patrol arrives. Ask questions somewhere safer.

没错。巡逻队赶来前跟我走。找个安全的地方再问。

**陆川**：The main station is nearby. Could we ask the staff for assistance there?

主车站就在附近。我们能去找工作人员帮忙吗？

**陈星遥**：Every entrance requires official permission. Without it, the gates will report your location.

每个入口都需要正式授权。没有权限，闸机会报告你的位置。

**陆川**：What is our alternative? This street seems blocked at both ends.

那还有什么办法？这条街的两端似乎都被封锁了。

**陈星遥**：An old maintenance passage runs below the shops. It leads to an abandoned platform.

商店下面有一条旧维修通道，通往废弃站台。

**陆川**：Does the passage have cameras as well? I would rather avoid another alarm.

通道里也有摄像头吗？我可不想再触发一次警报。

**陈星遥**：Not working ones. The equipment failed months ago. Nobody repaired it.

有，但早就坏了。设备几个月前就出了故障，也没人来修。

**旁白**：A steel door blocks the stairs. She checks the terminal, then shakes her head.

一扇铁门挡住楼梯。她检查了终端，随后摇了摇头。

**陈星遥**：The lock has power, but its control circuit is damaged. My tools cannot restore it.

锁还有电，但控制电路损坏了。我的工具修不好。

**万象系统**：Local fault identified. Remote repair available. Confirm activation to restore the damaged circuit.

已识别局部故障。远程修复可用。确认启动，即可恢复损坏的电路。

**陆川**：Confirmed. Stand back. I can handle this problem.

确认启动。离门远一点。我想，这个问题我能解决。

**陈星遥**：It is opening. You did not even touch the terminal. What exactly did you do?

门开了。你连终端都没碰。你到底做了什么？

**陆川**：Repaired the circuit. I will explain when we are no longer being followed.

修好了电路。等没人追我们的时候，我再解释。

**陈星遥**：Fair enough. I am Xingyao, a reporter. And I owe you an explanation too.

也对。我叫星遥，是记者。我也欠你一个解释。

**陆川**：Lu Chuan. If you are a reporter, why are you hiding from the patrol?

陆川。如果你是记者，为什么要躲着巡逻队？

**陈星遥**：I found evidence that citizens are losing their identities. My sister disappeared during the investigation.

我发现有居民的身份被删除。我妹妹在调查期间失踪了。

**陆川**：You believe those events are connected, but you still need proof. Is that right?

你认为这些事有联系，但还需要证据。对吗？

**陈星遥**：Yes. Help me reach the shelter. I will show you what I found.

对。帮我到地下避难所，我就给你看调查发现。

**旁白**：She offers her hand. Behind us, the door closes before the red light returns.

她向我伸出手。红光再次扫来之前，身后的门已经合上。

## 第一卷 · 霓虹失名者 / 第一章 1.2 地下末班车
废弃站台的身份闸机仍在追踪星遥。她能修好列车，却无法同时遮蔽沿线的巡逻信号。万象系统提示：部署信号干扰器，建立一次安全通行窗口。

**旁白**：The last train stands beneath the city, its windows dark and its doors locked.

末班列车停在城市地下，车窗漆黑，车门紧锁。

**陈星遥**：This line closed three years ago. The company said it was no longer profitable.

这条线路三年前停运。公司说它已经无法盈利。

**陆川**：Then why are the security gates still connected to the central network?

那为什么安检闸机还连着中央网络？

**陈星遥**：They track movement, not passengers. People without official records still need somewhere to go.

它们追踪移动，而不是乘客。没有正式记录的人仍然需要去处。

**万象系统**：Signal jammer deployed. Local surveillance will receive an empty platform image.

信号干扰器已部署。本地监控将接收到空站台影像。

**陆川**：So the cameras cannot identify us. Can we board the train now?

所以摄像头无法识别我们。现在能上车了吗？

**陈星遥**：Not yet. The main battery is damaged, but the emergency supply may still work.

还不行。主电池损坏了，不过应急电源可能还可以用。

**陆川**：You handle the power. I will keep the patrol signal away from this station.

你处理电源，我让巡逻信号远离这座车站。

**旁白**：Xingyao opens a metal panel. A thin blue light appears beneath her fingers.

星遥打开金属面板。她的指尖下亮起细细的蓝光。

**陈星遥**：The emergency battery has enough energy for one journey, provided we reduce the load.

应急电池足够跑一趟，前提是减轻负载。

**陆川**：Would turning off the lights help? We do not need the whole carriage illuminated.

关灯有用吗？我们不需要照亮整节车厢。

**陈星遥**：Yes, but heating consumes much more power. We should disconnect it first.

有用，但供暖消耗的电更多。应该先切断供暖。

**万象系统**：Heating disabled. Estimated travel range has increased by forty percent.

供暖已关闭。预计行驶距离增加百分之四十。

**陆川**：It will be cold. Take my jacket if you need it.

会很冷。如果需要，你可以披上我的外套。

**陈星遥**：Keep it. You have never spent a night underground. I can manage the temperature.

自己留着。你没在地下过过夜，我能应付这种温度。

**旁白**：She looks away, but moves her bag so that there is a seat beside her.

她移开目光，却挪开了包，给身边留出一个座位。

**陈星遥**：The shelter is beyond the next junction. We must take the maintenance tunnel.

避难所在下一个岔口后面。我们必须走维修隧道。

**陆川**：The public route looks shorter. Why not follow that one?

公共线路看起来更短。为什么不走那条？

**陈星遥**：Its final gate checks every passenger against the company database. Your name would be missing.

它的最后一道闸机会核对每位乘客的公司数据库记录。那里找不到你的名字。

**万象系统**：Alternative route confirmed. The maintenance tunnel has no identity checkpoint.

替代路线已确认。维修隧道没有身份检查点。

**陆川**：You knew this route before we met. Have you helped other people escape?

在遇见我之前，你就知道这条路。你帮过其他人逃走吗？

**陈星遥**：My father designed these tunnels. He disappeared after questioning the identity system.

这些隧道是我父亲设计的。他质疑身份系统后就失踪了。

**陆川**：Then we are not simply hiding. We are going to find out what happened to him.

那我们就不只是躲起来。我们要查清他究竟发生了什么。

**陈星遥**：There is an archive near the shelter. If his original reports survived, we may finally have evidence.

避难所附近有座档案室。如果他的原始报告还在，我们也许终于能找到证据。

## 第一卷 · 霓虹失名者 / 第一章 1.3 失名档案
档案室的公开终端只剩被篡改的摘要。星遥父亲留下的原始报告藏在离线存储层，普通设备无法辨认。万象系统提示：档案扫描器可以恢复记录结构并交叉核验时间戳。

**旁白**：The archive smells of dust and warm circuits. Most of its terminals have been disconnected.

档案室里混着灰尘和发热电路的气味。大部分终端都已断开。

**陈星遥**：The public report calls my father careless. It says his experiment caused the identity failures.

公开报告称父亲疏忽大意，说他的实验造成了身份故障。

**陆川**：A conclusion is not evidence. We need the observations that supported it.

结论不是证据。我们需要支撑它的原始观测。

**万象系统**：Archive scanner activated. Recovering an independent copy from offline storage.

档案扫描器已激活。正在从离线存储恢复独立副本。

**陈星遥**：Look at the dates. These complaints arrived two weeks before his experiment began.

看看日期。这些投诉在他的实验开始前两周就到了。

**陆川**：Then the failures cannot all be consequences of his work. They were already occurring.

那故障不可能全是他工作的结果。故障早已在发生。

**陈星遥**：The summary removed that detail. Someone wanted readers to blame him instead of the company.

摘要删掉了这个细节。有人想让读者责怪他，而不是公司。

**陆川**：Save the original files. We should not accuse anyone until we can verify their authenticity.

保存原始文件。在核实真实性之前，我们不应该指控任何人。

**万象系统**：Two sets of records located. Official totals differ from the raw transaction logs.

发现两组记录。官方总数与原始事务日志存在差异。

**陈星遥**：The official version lists only twelve people affected. The raw data contains hundreds.

官方版本只列出十二名受影响者。原始数据里有几百人。

**陆川**：Could repeated entries explain the difference? We should exclude duplicates before comparing them.

重复记录能解释差异吗？比较前应该先排除重复项。

**万象系统**：Duplicate entries excluded. Three hundred and eighteen distinct residents lost valid identification.

已排除重复记录。三百一十八名不同居民失去了有效身份。

**陈星遥**：So it was not a minor technical fault. Entire families lost access to housing and medical care.

所以这不是小技术故障。整个家庭失去了住房和医疗服务。

**陆川**：And reporting a smaller number made the problem seem less serious than it was.

而报告较小的数字，让问题显得没有实际那么严重。

**陈星遥**：My father requested an external investigation. The request was rejected without explanation.

父亲申请外部调查，却毫无解释地被拒绝了。

**陆川**：Keep that rejection too. It shows the company knew someone had challenged its account.

那份拒绝也留着。它表明公司知道有人质疑它的说法。

**旁白**：A photograph falls from a paper folder. Xingyao catches it before it touches the floor.

一张照片从纸质文件夹滑落。星遥在它落地前接住了它。

**陈星遥**：He wrote the storage address on the back. I thought it was just an old family photograph.

他把存储地址写在背面。我以为它只是一张旧全家福。

**陆川**：He left a way to check the evidence without relying on the central network.

他留下了一种不依赖中央网络的证据核验方法。

**万象系统**：Independent signatures match. No modification detected in the recovered source files.

独立签名一致。恢复的源文件未发现修改。

**陈星遥**：We should publish everything tonight. People deserve to know why their lives were erased.

我们今晚就应该全部公开。人们有权知道自己的生活为什么被抹去。

**陆川**：Yes, but remove personal addresses first. Evidence should protect the residents, not expose them.

是的，但要先删掉个人住址。证据应该保护居民，而不是暴露他们。

**陈星遥**：You are right. We can share the figures and verification method while keeping identities private.

你说得对。我们可以公开数字与核验方法，同时保护个人身份。

**旁白**：For the first time, she places the photograph beside my hand instead of hiding it in her coat.

她第一次把照片放到我的手边，而不是藏回大衣。

## 第一卷 · 霓虹失名者 / 第一章 1.4 黎明信号
中央网络随时可能删除证据。屋顶公共广播塔仍有一条独立通信线路，但旧协议已损坏。万象系统提示：协议修复芯片可以重建验证通道，让各社区同时收到可核验的记录。

**旁白**：Before sunrise, we reach the roof of a silent broadcast station overlooking the lower districts.

日出之前，我们来到沉寂广播站的屋顶，俯瞰下层城区。

**陈星遥**：The central network can remove a single upload in seconds. We need several independent destinations.

中央网络几秒内就能删除一份上传。我们需要多个独立目的地。

**陆川**：Could the community receivers distribute copies without asking the company for permission?

社区接收器能不经过公司批准就分发副本吗？

**陈星遥**：They could, if this tower still supported the original verification protocol. Its controller is corrupted.

如果这座塔仍支持原始核验协议，就可以。但控制器已经损坏。

**万象系统**：Protocol repair chip installed. Independent transmission channel restored.

协议修复芯片已安装。独立传输通道已恢复。

**陆川**：Then send the evidence to the community receivers first. A public copy should not have one point of failure.

那先把证据发给社区接收器。公开副本不应该只有一个故障点。

**陈星遥**：Once they hold separate copies, removing the original will no longer suppress the report.

一旦各处持有独立副本，删除原始上传也无法压住报告。

**旁白**：She connects her terminal. Across the sleeping city, small receiver lights begin to blink.

她接上终端。沉睡城市里的接收器开始闪烁。

**万象系统**：Warning. An automated instruction requests replacement of all existing identity records.

警告。自动指令要求替换全部现有身份记录。

**陈星遥**：That would restore the erased names, but it might also overwrite valid records. We cannot risk another disaster.

那会恢复被抹去的名字，也可能覆盖有效记录。我们不能再冒一次灾难的风险。

**陆川**：Restore only the entries verified by the independent archive, and preserve a copy of the current database.

只恢复经过独立档案核验的条目，并保留当前数据库副本。

**万象系统**：Targeted restoration prepared. Every proposed change has a recorded source and can be reversed.

定向恢复已准备。每项拟议修改都有来源记录，并可撤销。

**陈星遥**：My father argued for exactly this safeguard. They removed it because review slowed their operations.

父亲争取的就是这种保护机制。他们因为审核拖慢运营而移除了它。

**陆川**：Efficiency means little if people lose their rights whenever a machine makes an error.

如果机器一出错人们就失去权利，效率就没有多少意义。

**陈星遥**：The residents should be able to inspect and challenge decisions that affect them.

居民应该能够查看并质疑影响自己的决定。

**旁白**：The terminal asks for confirmation. This time, neither of us presses the button without reading.

终端要求确认。这一次，我们谁也没有不读内容就按下按钮。

**万象系统**：Evidence delivered. Targeted restoration completed. Independent review requests are now available.

证据已送达。定向恢复已完成。独立复核申请现已开放。

**陈星遥**：They have their names back. But a restored record cannot return the years they spent excluded.

他们拿回了名字。但恢复记录无法归还那些被排除在外的岁月。

**陆川**：No. It gives them a starting point, and the evidence gives them a way to demand accountability.

是的。它给了他们一个起点，而证据让他们有办法要求追责。

**陈星遥**：A message from a distant district mentions my father. Someone remembers seeing him after the investigation.

远方城区的一条消息提到了父亲。有人记得在调查之后见过他。

**陆川**：Then our next journey has a destination. We can follow that lead after you get some rest.

那我们的下一段旅程就有了目的地。等你休息一下，我们就去追这条线索。

**陈星遥**：You keep saying we. You could have used your system to leave this city alone.

你一直说“我们”。你本来可以用系统独自离开这座城市。

**陆川**：I was preparing for an exam when I arrived here. Helping you was not on the schedule, but I am staying.

来到这里之前，我还在备考。帮你不在原来的计划里，但我会留下。

**旁白**：She smiles as daylight reaches the tower. For now, the city has remembered its missing names.

晨光照上广播塔，她笑了。至少此刻，城市重新记起了那些失落的名字。
