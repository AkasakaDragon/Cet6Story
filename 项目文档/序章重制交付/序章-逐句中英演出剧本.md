## 还有一页

原世界 · 晚上 · 下雨

**序-001｜时间字幕：** 原世界，晚上。窗外的雨已经下了很久。

Original world. Late evening. Rain has been falling for hours.

**序-002｜室友：** 你那盏灯还不关？我睡醒一小觉了，睁眼看见你还在，以为天亮了。

Is your lamp still on? I woke up from a short nap and thought it was morning.

**序-003｜陆川：** 就剩这一页。答案对了，但这份解析写得绕，我想知道它究竟绕在哪里。

Just one page left. My answer is correct, but the explanation is confusing. I want to understand why.

**序-004｜室友：** 做对了还跟解析较劲？换我早把书合上了。

You got it right and still argue with the explanation? I would have closed the book.

**序-005｜陆川：** 做对是一回事，弄明白是另一回事。不然下次换个问法，我还得碰运气。

Getting it right is one thing; understanding it is another. Otherwise, I will depend on luck next time.

**序-006｜室友：** 那你顺便研究一下，为什么明天第一节课不能晚半小时。

Then investigate why tomorrow's first class cannot start half an hour later.

**序-007｜陆川：** 这个超出教材范围。你写信给排课老师，我帮你检查语法。

That is beyond the textbook. Write to the timetable office; I can check your grammar.

**序-008｜室友：** 算了，帮我带两个包子实际些。窗关严一点，刚才雨都飘进来了。

Never mind. Bring me two steamed buns instead. Close the window properly; some rain came in.

**序-009｜陆川：** 两个，记住了。你先睡，我看完这页就关灯。

Two. Noted. Get some sleep. I will switch off the lamp after this page.

**序-010｜旁白：** 床帘后传来含糊的答应。陆川把杯子挪开，桌上的电脑忽然闪了一下。

A sleepy reply comes from behind the curtain. Lu Chuan moves his cup aside. The laptop flickers.

## 女神的请求

交界空间 · 时间未明 · 无天气

【CG】cg03

**序-020｜时间字幕：** 交界空间。这里没有正常的昼夜，原世界的雨声已经消失。

The boundary between worlds. There is no ordinary day or night here. The rain is silent.

**序-021｜陆川：** 这不是我的衣服……刚才明明还在桌前。有人能告诉我，发生了什么吗？

These are not my clothes. I was at my desk. Can someone explain what happened?

**序-022｜伊瑟雅：** 先放松手，你没有受伤。我是伊瑟雅，守护艾瑟兰地脉联系的女神。是我发起了召唤。

Relax your hand. You are not injured. I am Iserya, guardian of Aetherlan's connected lifelines. I summoned you.

**序-023｜陆川：** 你用了“召唤”，但我还没有答应。我的家人和同学都在那边。

You say you summoned me, but I did not agree. My family and friends are still there.

**序-024｜伊瑟雅：** 你说得对。你的出现不等于同意。我不会要求你现在就宣誓。

You are right. Your presence is not consent. I will not ask for an oath.

**序-025｜陆川：** 那先说最实际的问题。我能回去吗？

Then let us start with the practical question. Can I go home?

**序-026｜伊瑟雅：** 能，但不是现在。归途经过大陆的地脉，污染正在侵蚀它。强行开启，会伤害你。

Yes, but not yet. The way home crosses the continent's lifelines. Pollution is damaging them. Opening it now could hurt you.

**序-027｜陆川：** 送我来时为什么可以？来和回，总该有个区别。

Why was bringing me here possible? There must be a difference between arriving and returning.

**序-028｜伊瑟雅：** 我仅存的跨界力量，只够建立一次单向召唤。现在，我也无法再召来第二个人。

My remaining power could create only one passage in one direction. I cannot summon another person now.

**序-029｜陆川：** 所以我先要了解这里出了什么事。别只告诉我，你需要一个英雄。

Then I need to understand what happened here. Please do not simply tell me you need a hero.

**序-030｜伊瑟雅：** 八十年前，魔王瓦尔莫斯从古老遗迹附近出现。他烧毁村庄，侵占城镇，制造自己的军队。

Eighty years ago, the demon king Valmors appeared near ancient ruins. He destroyed villages, occupied towns, and created an army.

**序-031｜陆川：** 既然他现在被封印，说明有人挡住过他。

If he is sealed now, someone must have stopped him.

**序-032｜伊瑟雅：** 奥德里克二世国王的魔术师赛维恩，用毕生研究铸成五颗宝石，交给五位将军。

King Oderic the Second's magician, Severn, used a lifetime of research to forge five gems for five generals.

**序-033｜伊瑟雅：** 他们打了十年的仗，收复土地，最后把瓦尔莫斯封印在他建于古庭之上的堡垒中。

They fought for ten years, reclaimed the land, and sealed Valmors in the fortress he built over the ancient court.

**序-034｜陆川：** 十年才换来的胜利。为什么今天又出了问题？

A victory that took ten years. Why is it failing now?

**序-035｜伊瑟雅：** 战后，宝石深埋在五处制造魔物的地区，压住污染。封印已经持续七十年，力量正在衰弱。

After the war, the gems were buried in five regions where monsters were made. They contained the pollution for seventy years. Their power is fading.

**序-036｜伊瑟雅：** 城镇传来失常的钟声，森林的动物开始发狂。码头、陨坑和矿洞，也陆续出现异常。

The town's bells sound wrongly. Forest animals turn violent. The docks, the crater, and the mines also show signs of danger.

**序-037｜陆川：** 这不是五场碰巧一起发生的灾害，而是同一条路出了问题。

These are not five unrelated disasters. Something connecting them is failing.

**序-038｜伊瑟雅：** 是的。归灯驿站曾是五条维护线路汇合的地方。它的设施，还能让宝石恢复稳定。

Yes. Guidelight Waystation once connected five maintenance routes. Its equipment can still restore the gems to stability.

**序-039｜陆川：** 我听见“驿站”，不是“军营”。我要先修一个能住人的地方？

You said a waystation, not a military camp. I need to repair a place where people can stay?

**序-040｜伊瑟雅：** 有人能休息、补给、交换消息，维护才不会只靠几个人苦撑。宝石需要设施，也需要懂得照料它们的人。

People need rest, supplies, and reliable information. Maintenance cannot depend on a few exhausted people. The gems need equipment and people who understand it.

**序-041｜陆川：** 我不会拿剑，也没有经营经验。这个计划里，你至少得留出让我学的时间。

I cannot fight with a sword or run an inn. Your plan must allow me time to learn.

**序-042｜伊瑟雅：** 我会给你雷电的力量。它通过吟唱释放，但不能替你判断安全的位置。

I will grant you the power of lightning. Chanting releases it, but it cannot choose a safe place for you.

**序-043｜陆川：** 雷电。听起来很有用，也很容易先把自己劈了。

Lightning. Useful, but it sounds rather easy to strike myself first.

**序-044｜伊瑟雅：** 先选目标，再保持呼吸，说清楚完整的咒句。紧张时可以停下重来，不能含糊地硬撑。

Choose your target, breathe steadily, and speak the whole chant clearly. If frightened, stop and begin again. Do not force it.

**序-045｜伊瑟雅：** “Gather, lightning. Follow my voice.”先让它只在指尖亮起。

Gather, lightning. Follow my voice. First, let it shine only at your fingertips.

**序-046｜陆川：** Gather, lightning. Follow my voice.……真的亮了。还好，你没让我第一下就打那扇门。

Gather, lightning. Follow my voice. It really shines. I am glad my first attempt was not aimed at that door.

**序-047｜伊瑟雅：** 水、金属和同伴的位置，会改变它经过的路线。记住，你可以先问，不必急着证明自己。

Water, metal, and nearby companions change its path. Remember: ask first. You do not need to prove yourself immediately.

**序-048｜陆川：** 你不能保证我一路顺利。但如果归途需要修复，我愿意先找到能一起做事的人。

You cannot promise an easy journey. But if the way home needs repair, I will look for people who can help.

**序-049｜伊瑟雅：** 到达以后，先找安全的屋檐。有人正在巡查那条路。你不会因为暂时不知道答案，就失去求助的资格。

When you arrive, find safe shelter first. Someone is patrolling that road. Not knowing the answer does not mean you cannot ask for help.

If you want to thank me, move your wet cloak. It is covering the last dry piece of wood.

**序-101｜陆川：** 好的，骑士。我发现你的规矩多数都跟保住今晚有关。

Of course, knight. Most of your rules seem to be about surviving tonight.

**序-102｜艾莉娅：** 规矩应该有理由。你不明白时可以问，紧急的时候先照做。

Rules should have reasons. Ask when you do not understand. In an emergency, act first.

**序-103｜陆川：** 那也约定一条：你撑不住的时候告诉我，别把所有事都当成你的职责。

Then let us agree on one more: tell me when you are tired. Not everything has to be your responsibility.

**序-104｜艾莉娅：** ……可以。先休息两个时辰，我守前半段，后半段叫你。

All right. Rest for a few hours. I will take the first watch and wake you for the next.

## 把门留给明天

归灯驿站 · 第2天 · 早上 · 多云

**序-105｜时间字幕：** 一夜过去。第2天早上，雨停了，厚云仍遮着天空。

A night passes. Day two, morning. The rain has stopped, but thick clouds remain.

**序-106｜旁白：** 临时补板撑过了夜雨。壁炉边的地面干了，屋顶缺口和封住的楼梯仍清楚可见。

The temporary boards held through the rain. The floor by the hearth is dry; the roof damage and blocked stairs remain.

**序-107｜陆川：** 我昨晚梦见自己回去上课。醒来发现不用迟到，又觉得好像没那么值得高兴。

I dreamed I was going to class. Waking up without needing to rush is less comforting than I expected.

**序-108｜艾莉娅：** 想家很正常。先喝水，我们吃一点干粮，再去村庄。

Missing home is normal. Drink some water. We will eat a little and go to the village.

**序-109｜陆川：** 这里没有突然变成一家好酒馆。但至少这盏灯还在。

This has not suddenly become a good inn. But at least the lamp is still here.

**序-110｜艾莉娅：** 灯油够到今晚。别先承诺接待客人，厨房、用水和屋顶都要验收。

There is oil until tonight. Do not promise rooms yet. The kitchen, water, and roof need inspection.

**序-111｜陆川：** 明白。今天先去找能一起修的人，老板的招牌等事情做成再挂。

Understood. Today, find people who can help repair it. The owner's sign can wait until the work is done.

**序-112｜艾莉娅：** 带上那根铜线的拓印。村里有人见过旧维护设施。

Bring a copy of the marks on that copper wire. Someone in the village knows the old equipment.

**序-113｜陆川：** 走吧。先弄清五道刻痕，也先弄清早餐能在哪里买。

Let us go. Find out about those five marks, and where to get breakfast.

**序-114｜旁白：** 两人拉好门栓，朝村庄走去。归灯驿站的第一夜结束，真正的重建才刚要开始。

They secure the door and walk towards the village. Guidelight's first night is over. Its real rebuilding is about to begin.
